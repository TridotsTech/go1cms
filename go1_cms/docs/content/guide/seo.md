# SEO

SEO helps your pages show up well in search results and when links are shared.

## How to open it

Choose **SEO** in the **Go live** group of the nav rail, then choose the **Website** at the top.

## What you can do here

1. **See the big picture.** **Pages at a glance** lists each page with its **Score** and the number of **Issues**. **What would lift the score most** points at the quickest wins.
2. **Edit a page's SEO.** Choose **Edit SEO** on a page. Under **Search result** set the **Search title**, **Description**, **Focus keyphrase** and **Keywords**.
3. **Control indexing.** Under **Search engines** you can switch **Show this page in search results** off (Google drops the page and it leaves the sitemap) or stop search engines following its links. Leave these on unless you have a reason.
4. **Set the main address.** **Main address (canonical)** is for when the same page lives at two addresses. Leave it empty if the page is its own main address.
5. **Shape shared links.** Under **When the link is shared**, set the **Share title**, **Share description** and **Share image (1200×630)**.
6. **Check rich results.** The panel shows what search engines could read from the page.
7. **Use the sitemap.** **XML Sitemap** has **Open sitemap** and **Copy URL**, to give to Google Search Console.

> [!NOTE]
> Members-only pages are never shown to search engines, whatever you set.
