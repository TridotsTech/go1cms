# Menus

Menus builds the navigation visitors see: the header links, the footer links and dropdowns.

## How to open it

Choose **Menus** in the **Build** group of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Pick a project and a menu.** Choose the project, then the menu from the list. **New** makes another one; **Create your first menu** appears when there is none.
2. **Add items.** Choose **Add item** and set its **Label** and **Link**. **Add first item** starts an empty menu.
3. **Start from your pages.** **Suggest from pages** drafts a menu from the pages you already have. **Paste a list** or **Edit as list** lets you type the whole menu as an indented list.
4. **Reorder and nest.** Drag an item to reorder it; drop it on the middle of another item to nest it as a sub-item.
5. **Fine-tune one item.** Select an item to open **Item settings**: **Visible**, **Position** (left or right), **Mega menu** (multi-column dropdown), an icon, a **Badge**, a **Description**, and who it is **Visible to** (everyone, signed-out visitors, signed-in members, or chosen roles).
6. **Fill sub-items automatically.** **Fill sub-items automatically** keeps a dropdown up to date with new pages under an address, or the latest blog posts.
7. **Preview.** **Preview** shows how the menu will look.

## Common questions

**A hidden item is still in the list.** Yes. Hidden items stay here but are not shown to visitors.

**What does "Unlinked items" mean?** An item whose link points nowhere. Fix its **Link**.
