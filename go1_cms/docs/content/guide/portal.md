# Portal

Portal turns a website into a members' area: people sign in, see their own pages and records, and can pay or book. Use it for client portals, staff areas or small web apps.

## How to open it

Choose **Portal** in the **Apps** group of the nav rail, then pick the website at the top right.

## What you can do here

1. **Set the website type.** Under **Website and sign-up**, choose **Kind of website**: Website, Portal or Web App. Decide who can join with **New members**: **Open** (anyone), **Approval** (sign-ups wait for you) or **Invite only** (you add people).
2. **Choose the members' area.** Set **After sign-in go to**, and whether members' pages look like an **App layout** or the **Website pages**.
3. **Add roles.** Under **Roles**, choose **+ Add role** and say what each is for.
4. **Add members.** Choose **Invite someone**, enter an **Email** and **Name**, and choose **Add member**. Send them the link shown to set their password. The member list shows who is **Waiting**, **Active** or **Switched off**.
5. **Share your records.** Under data sources choose **+ New data source** or **Add a ready one…**. For each, decide **Who can read** and **Which records** they see (everyone, or only their own), and what members can do.
6. **Take payments.** Under **Online payments**, add your Razorpay keys. Secrets are stored encrypted and never shown again.
7. **Let members install the app.** Under **Installable app**, set the app name, colour and icon.
8. **Check before you launch.** **Preview as** shows the website as a visitor, a role or one member would see it, with nothing changeable. The audit list shows what happened and when, and the safety check lists findings to fix.

> [!NOTE]
> A website is not a portal until its kind is set to **Portal** in Settings.
