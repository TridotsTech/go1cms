# Home

Home is the first screen after you sign in. It is where you start a new website or pick up where you left off.

## How to open it

Choose **Home** at the top of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Describe the website you want.** Type a sentence about your business in the box that says "Describe the website you want to build…" and press **Build** (or Enter). The AI builder drafts a site you can then edit.
2. **Start with a template.** Under "Start with a template" you see full multi-page websites, each editable section by section. Choose one, or pick **Browse all** to see the whole set.
3. **Open your workspace.** If you already have websites, use **Open workspace** to go to your projects.

## Common questions

**I built something I do not like. Is it live?** No. A new website is a draft until you publish it. See [Publish](../publish).

**Can I change the template's colours afterwards?** Yes. Open [Theme](../theme) and change colours and fonts for the whole site.

## Where to go next

[Projects and pages](../projects) shows how to manage the pages of what you just made.
