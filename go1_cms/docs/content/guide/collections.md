# Collections and content types

Collections are lists of content your pages can show: products, team members, events, testimonials. Content types let you make your own kinds of list.

## How to open it

Choose **Collections** in the **Content** group of the nav rail. The **Content types** tab sits inside it.

## Collections

1. **Pick a collection.** Search with "Search collections…" and click one to see its entries. **New collection** makes another.
2. **Add an entry.** Choose **Add entry** (or **Add the first entry** when it is empty) and fill in the fields.
3. **Choose the columns.** Use **Columns** to pick what the table shows. Switch between **Table** and **Cards**.
4. **Move data in and out.** **Spreadsheet** offers **Import from a file…**, **Download as Excel** and **Download as CSV**.
5. **See where it shows.** **Where this collection appears** lists the pages that use it.

## Content types

1. Choose **New content type** (for example "Events") and name it, with an address word for its pages.
2. Add extra fields with **Add field**. Every entry also has a title, an address, a summary, an image, a website and Published.
3. **Create pages** makes the list and detail pages for the type. Choose **Publish them now** only if you want them live straight away; otherwise they open as drafts.
4. **Write entries in Collections** takes you to the entries.

> [!NOTE]
> Removing a field or deleting a content type is permanent. The kind of a saved field cannot change; make a new field instead.
