# Posts

Posts is your blog: write articles, schedule them, and publish them on the website.

## How to open it

Choose **Posts** in the **Content** group of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Find a post.** Search with "Search posts…", or filter by **Website**. **All my websites** shows everything.
2. **Start a post.** Choose **New post**. Pick **Blank post**, a template under **Templates**, or **Write with AI** (describe the post, add points to include and a length; review the draft before anything is published).
3. **Write.** Give it a title and type in "Start writing…". You can add an **Image from library**.
4. **Set it up.** In **Publishing**, choose the **Website**, the **Address** and a **Publish date**. A future date keeps a published post off the website until then. Tick **Featured (shown first)** or **Allow comments** if you want them.
5. **Organise.** Under **Summary and organisation** set the **Excerpt**, **Category**, **Tags** and **Author**.
6. **Help search engines.** Under **Search engines**, set the **Search title**, **Search description** and **Share image**. Without a share image, the cover image is used.
7. **Publish.** Choose **Publish**; **Unpublish** takes it down again. **View** opens it on the website.

## Common questions

**The post is not on my site.** Check the **Publish date** is not in the future and that you chose a **Website**.

**Can I reuse a layout?** Yes. **Save this layout as a template** and new posts on that website can start from it.

> [!NOTE]
> AI drafts are written in the website's language. Nothing is published until you choose to.
