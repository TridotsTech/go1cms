# Analytics

Analytics shows how many people visit your website, what they look at, and where they come from.

## How to open it

Choose **Analytics** in the **Go live** group of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Choose what to look at.** Pick the **Project** (or **All projects**), a **Page**, a **Date range** and the **Visitor type**.
2. **Read the headline numbers.** **Unique Visitors** and **Total Pageviews** sit at the top, with a chart of traffic over time. The **Highlights** are worked out from those numbers.
3. **Dig in.** See **Top pages**, **Sources**, **Devices**, **Operating systems**, **Browsers**, **Countries** and **Referrers**.
4. **Set goals.** Choose **New goal** and give it a name (for example "Contact form sent") to count a win. Goals belong to one website, so pick a project first.
5. **Get a weekly report.** **Email this week's report now** sends the summary; the settings next to it hold the ad pixels and weekly email.
6. **Connect Google Analytics.** **Configure GA4** takes the property ID and service account credentials, then **Save Settings**.
7. **Export.** **Export** downloads the data.

## Common questions

**There is no data.** A new website has none until visitors arrive and its pages are published. "No analytics data for this period yet" means just that. See [Publish](../publish).
