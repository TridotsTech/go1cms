# Popups

Popups are messages that appear over a page at the right moment: an offer, a sign-up, an announcement.

## How to open it

Choose **Popups** in the **Go live** group of the nav rail, then choose the website.

## What you can do here

1. **Make one.** Choose **New popup**, give it a **Name** (for example "Spring sale") and choose what to **Start from**. It opens on the canvas, switched off.
2. **Design it.** Choose **Edit design** to open it in the builder. Publish it there when it is ready; Publish also switches it on.
3. **Set when it appears.** **Settings** holds who sees it and when.
4. **Watch it work.** The list shows each popup's **Status**, how often it was **Seen** and **Clicked**. **Results** has the detail.
5. **Copy or remove.** **Make a copy** duplicates a popup; **Delete** removes it.

> [!NOTE]
> A popup with the tag "unpublished edits" has changes in the editor that are not live yet. Publish in the editor to send them.
