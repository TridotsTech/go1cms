# Theme

Theme Studio sets the colours, fonts, spacing and corners of a whole website, so every page stays on-brand.

## How to open it

Choose **Theme** in the **Build** group of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Pick a project.** Use the **Project** menu at the top. Turn on **Use Theme Studio on Website** to let that website follow your theme.
2. **Choose a design system.** Under **Design system**, browse the ready-made looks (**Browse all**) and click one to preview it. Nothing changes on your pages yet.
3. **Make it yours.** Under **Tokens**, change the **Brand colour**, **Typography** (display and body fonts, type scale), **Color palette**, spacing, **Radius** and visual effects.
4. **Check readability.** **Readability** warns when text is hard to read on its background, and **Fix** corrects it.
5. **Match a website.** Paste a website address under **Match a website** to read its colours and fonts into a theme.
6. **Apply.** **Apply this page** restyles one page; **Apply to all pages** restyles the whole website.
7. **Undo.** **History** (Restyle history) lets you step back a restyle. **Clear theme** removes the applied look from a page.
8. **Save and share.** **Save as my theme** keeps it under My themes for every project; **Export theme** and **Import theme** move themes between sites.

> [!NOTE]
> Choosing a design system is a preview only. Your pages change when you choose **Apply this page** or **Apply to all pages**.
