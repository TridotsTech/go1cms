# Docs

Docs is where help articles are written: the guides your own customers or team read, like this one.

## How to open it

Press ⌘K and type "Docs", or choose **Docs** at the bottom of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Pick a space.** A space is one set of articles. The **Space** menu switches between them; **New space** adds one and **Space settings** edits it.
2. **Find an article.** Use "Filter articles" and the list on the left. Drag to reorder; drop an article on the middle of another to nest it under it.
3. **Write.** Choose **New article**, then fill in the title and the **Address**, and write in the editor. The preview on the right appears as you write and is exactly what publishes.
4. **Organise.** Set its **Group** in the sidebar, put it **Under article** another, and add **Tags** and a **Version**.
5. **Publish.** **Publish** makes it visible; **Unpublish** returns it to draft. **Open the docs site** shows what readers see.
6. **Copy or remove.** Use **Duplicate** and **Delete** from the article's menu.
