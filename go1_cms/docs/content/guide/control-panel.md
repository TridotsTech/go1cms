# The control panel

The control panel is the hosted account area for your plan, websites, domains, team and billing. It is separate from the builder, and some screens only appear if your account has them.

## How to open it

The control panel lives at `/cms/panel` on the site that runs it (older links to `/panel` forward there). Its screens are Websites, Domains, Team and Billing. In the quick finder (⌘K) you can also choose **Create a website** or **Add a domain**.

## Websites

Shows your **Plan**, then the **Websites of this account**, with **Usage** and **Domains** for each. Choose **Create website**, give it a **Website name**, a **Web address** and a **Plan**. **Open builder** goes straight into the builder with no second sign-in. A site shows "setting up…" for a short while after it is created. You may be asked to **Verify your email** before you create your first one.

## Domains

A domain belongs to a website, so create the website first, then connect the domain. A website's page also shows its **Live at** address (the platform address) and **Domains & publishing**.

## Team

Shows the **Members** of your team and the **Billing details** that appear on every invoice. Only an Owner or Admin can edit the billing address.

## Billing

Shows your **Current plan** and the **Plans** you can move to, with **Pay now**, **Cancel at period end**, and your **Invoices** to download as PDF.

## Admin (operators only)

The **Ops** screen is for the people who run the platform: reviewers, user accounts, all sites and dangerous actions such as suspending a site. It asks you to type the site name to confirm anything destructive. Site owners do not see it.

> [!NOTE]
> What you can do depends on your role and plan. If a button is missing, ask the account Owner.
