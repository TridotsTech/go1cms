# Glossary

The words used across the admin, in plain language.

## Words you will meet

**Project (or website).** One website with all its pages, theme, menus, posts and forms. You pick a project at the top of many screens. See [Projects and pages](../projects).

**Page.** One address on your site, such as the home page or the contact page. A page is a stack of sections.

**Section.** A horizontal band of a page: a header, a row of three features, a price table, a footer. You add, move and edit sections in the builder. See [The page builder](../builder).

**Component.** A section or block you saved so you can use it again. Change it once and you can update every place it is used.

**Library.** The shelf of ready-made sections and whole-website templates. See [Library](../library).

**Collection.** A list of things your pages can show, such as products, team members or events. See [Collections and content types](../collections).

**Theme.** Your colours, fonts, spacing and corner shapes in one place, so every page looks like the same brand. See [Theme](../theme).

**Draft.** Work that only you can see. Saving keeps a draft; visitors see nothing new.

**Publish.** The step that makes your draft visible to visitors. Until you publish, the live site stays as it was. See [Publish](../publish).

**Unpublish.** Takes a page back offline. The page and its history are kept.

**Form.** A set of questions visitors fill in, such as a contact form. Answers arrive in the form's inbox. See [Forms](../forms).

**Plugin.** An extra feature you switch on for a website, such as chat or a shop. See [Plugins](../plugins).

**Portal.** A website where members sign in and see their own pages and records. See [Portal](../portal).
