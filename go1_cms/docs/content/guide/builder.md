# The page builder

The builder is where you design a page: you see the page as visitors will, and click anything to change it.

## How to open it

In [Projects](../projects), choose **Edit** on a page. The builder takes over the window; the back button at the top left returns you to your pages.

## The layout

- **Top bar.** The back button, the page title and its address, **Design** and **AI** mode, undo and redo, the device sizes (desktop, tablet, phone), a preview eye, the save status, and **Publish**.
- **Left side.** Three tabs: **Pages** (switch to another page of the project), **Layers** (the page as a tree of sections and parts) and **Theme** (quick colour and font changes).
- **Right side.** Three tabs for the selected item: **Design** (layout, spacing, colour, effects), **Content** (the text, image or link) and **Settings**.
- **Middle.** The canvas, with a zoom control at the bottom.

## What you can do here

1. **Select and change.** Click a section or an item on the canvas, then edit it on the right.
2. **Edit text in place.** Double-click text on the canvas and type; a small toolbar appears for bold, italic, links and headings.
3. **Add things.** Use the add buttons in the top bar to insert sections, elements, shapes and text. Whole sections also come from the [Library](../library).
4. **Check other screens.** Switch the device size to see the tablet and phone layouts.
5. **Preview.** Use the eye to see the page without the editing tools.
6. **Go live.** Choose **Publish** when you are happy.

## Saving

The builder saves by itself a couple of seconds after you stop typing, and the top bar shows **Saved** once it has. Saving only keeps your draft; visitors see the change when you publish.

> [!NOTE]
> Made a mistake? Use undo (Ctrl or ⌘ + Z) and redo in the top bar.
