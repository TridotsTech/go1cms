# Forms

Forms collects enquiries, bookings and feedback. Visitors fill in a form on your site; the answers land in the form's inbox, and can be emailed to you.

## How to open it

Choose **Forms** in the **Content** group of the nav rail. Each row shows the number of **Responses** and the **Last response**.

## Make a form

1. Choose **New form**. Pick a template, or **Blank form**.
2. Give it a **Form name** (visitors see it as the heading) and choose its **Website**. Choose **Create form**.

## The editor tabs

**Questions.** Add and arrange questions with **Add a question**, then click one to edit its label, placeholder, options, width and whether it is required. Besides short and long text, choices and dates there are:
- **File upload**, for attachments. Common file types up to 10 MB are accepted; others are refused with a clear message.
- **Recommend score**, a 0 to 10 scale.
- **Face rating**, five faces from sad to happy.
- **Choice cards**, tiles to pick from.

Each question has an **Answer key**, used in emails and when saving records.

**Emails.** Under **Tell your team**, choose who gets an email for each response, or a morning summary, and use **Send me a test**. You can route to different people by an answer. **Thank-you email to the visitor** replies automatically; it needs an Email question on the form.

**Settings.** Choose where the form appears (**Copy link**, **Put it on your website**), what happens **After someone sends the form** (a message or another page), and whether to **Also save responses as records**. **Send responses to other apps** posts each response to an address starting with https://; **Send a test** checks it. **Spam check** adds a simple sum question for visitors. **Delete this form** removes it; its responses stay in the inbox.

**Responses.** The inbox. Open a response to read it and see whether it reached other apps. Deleting a response asks if you want to keep the saved record and delete only the inbox entry.

## From the forms list

The **⋯** menu on each form offers **Edit questions**, **Email settings**, **Open form**, **Copy link**, **Embed code** (paste it into any other website), **Make a copy** and **Delete**.

## Common questions

**What is the red "Can't save responses" tag?** The form saves answers as a record, but it has no question for something that record requires. Add those questions, or choose **Don't create records**. Visitors cannot send the form until it is fixed.

**I get no emails.** Email sending must be set up on the site. The Emails tab says when it is not.

**Do bots fill my form?** A hidden trap quietly drops them; they see "thank you" but nothing is stored.
