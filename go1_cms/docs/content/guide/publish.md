# Publish

Publish shows what is live on your website and the history of what you published. Making a page live happens in [Projects](../projects) or the [page builder](../builder); this screen is where you check the result.

## How to open it

Choose **Publish** in the **Go live** group of the nav rail.

## What you can see here

1. **Live.** The website's address and how many pages are currently published. **Refresh** reloads the numbers.
2. **Publish history.** When pages were last published. It reads "No publishes recorded yet" until you publish for the first time.
3. **Recent page edits.** Pages changed since, which are not live until you publish them.
4. **Domains and environments.** The domains attached to the site and where it runs.
5. **Settings.** Opens the publish settings.

## How to put a page live

1. Open the page in the [builder](../builder) and choose **Publish**. Or choose **Publish** on its card in [Projects](../projects), which can also publish several pages together.
2. To take a page down, choose **Unpublish**.

## Common questions

**I saved, but visitors do not see it.** Saving keeps a draft. Publish the page.

**Where do I connect my own domain?** In the control panel. See [The control panel](../control-panel).
