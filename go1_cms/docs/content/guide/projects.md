# Projects and pages

Projects is where every website and every page lives. Each project is one website; open a project to see its pages.

## How to open it

Choose **Projects** in the **Build** group of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Make a project.** Choose **New project**, type a name such as "Acme Bakery" and create it. It starts with a blank first page.
2. **Open a project.** Projects show as folders. Click one (or **Open folder**) to see its pages. **All folders** takes you back.
3. **Add a page.** Choose **New page**, then fill in the page title and address, and choose what to **Start from**: **Blank**, a template, a component, or **Generate with AI**. A **Landing page** option gives you an offer, a form and a thank-you page with no menu.
4. **Work on a page.** Each page card has **Edit**, **Preview**, **Publish** or **Unpublish**, **Duplicate**, **Delete** and a **More** menu with **Rename**, **Move to…**, **Save as template**, **Parent page…**, **Password…**, **Campaign & end date…** and **A/B test…**.
5. **Handle many pages at once.** Tick pages with **Select**, or use **Select all shown**, then publish, unpublish, move or delete them together.
6. **Find pages.** Use the search box, the **All tags** filter, and the grid or list view toggle.

## Things worth knowing

- **Rename** changes the page's title only; the address stays the same, so existing links keep working.
- **Password…** asks visitors for a password. Tick the option to protect its child pages too.
- **Deleted pages go to the Bin** and stay there for 30 days. Open **Bin**, then **Restore** to bring one back as a draft, with its history.
- **Members** and **Portal** buttons open who can edit the website and the members' area. See [Portal](../portal).
- **Save as template** (project level) turns a whole website into a reusable template.

> [!NOTE]
> A new page always belongs to a project. If you have none yet, the New page window lets you create one first.
