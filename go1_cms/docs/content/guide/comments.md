# Comments

Comments is the inbox for what readers write under your posts.

## How to open it

Choose **Comments** in the **Content** group of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Find comments.** Search with "Search comments…" or filter by **Website**. **All my websites** shows all.
2. **Approve.** Choose **Publish** on a comment to show it on the post. **Unpublish** hides it again.
3. **Reply.** Choose **Reply**, type under **Your reply** and send it.
4. **Mark spam.** **Spam** moves a comment out of sight; **Not spam** brings it back.
5. **Clean up.** **Delete** removes a comment; use **Select all** to act on several at once.

## Common questions

**Why do I see no comments?** Comments only arrive on posts where **Allow comments** is ticked. See [Posts](../posts).
