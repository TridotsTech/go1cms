# Library

The Library is a shelf of ready-made sections and whole-website templates that you can add to your pages.

## How to open it

Choose **Library** in the **Build** group of the nav rail.

## What you can do here

1. **Browse.** Pick a **Category** or **Industry**, or search with "Search sections…".
2. **Look closer.** Click a section to see its **Details** and a preview.
3. **Filter by source or tag.** Use **Source** and **Tags** to narrow the list, and **Project** to show the sections kept for one website.
4. **Insert.** Choose **Insert** to add a section to a page.
5. **Manage your own.** In **Manage** you can rename a section, file it, tag it, make a copy or delete it. A block can be kept to one project (it is then offered only while you build that project) or made available to **Every project**.
6. **Get new sections.** **Get latest sections** pulls in the newest ones.

## Common questions

**I do not see my section.** Clear the filters with **Clear all filters**; one of them may be hiding it.

## Where to go next
