# Get started

From nothing to a published page takes about ten minutes on the hosted service.

## 1. Create an account

Go to [Sign up](/signup), pick a plan and enter your name, email and a password. Starter is free and needs no card. Paid plans start a 14-day trial.

After sign-up you land in the **control panel** at `/panel`. Verify your email there: press **Send code**, enter the six-digit code from your inbox, and press **Verify**.

## 2. Create a website

Press **Create website**. Give it a name and a web address. The address is a label under `go1cms.site`, checked as you type. On Starter your website is ready in seconds; on Medium and Premium it takes a few minutes while your own site is provisioned.

Press **Open builder**. The control panel signs you into your website and opens its pages.

## 3. Build a page

Your website starts with a blank home page. From the Pages screen open it in the builder.

- Press the **+** in the header to open the insert panel: whole-site **Templates**, single **Sections** and raw **Elements**.
- Add a section, click text to edit it, and use the inspector on the right for layout, type, colour and spacing.
- Switch device at the top of the canvas to check tablet and phone.

[Builder basics](/docs/builder-basics) walks through the screen in detail.

## 4. Publish

Press **Publish**. Your page goes live at your web address with server-side rendering and a versioned snapshot you can roll back to. [Publish your site](/docs/publish) covers domains, rollback and previews.
