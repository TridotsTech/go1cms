# Plans and limits

The hosted service has three plans. Self-hosted installations have no limits.

| Limit | Starter | Medium | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting | project on a shared site | your own site, shared server | your own site, dedicated server |
| Price | Free | ₹2,499 / month | ₹7,999 / month + server |
| Websites | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Pages per website | 10 | 100 | Unlimited |
| Storage | 250 MB | 10 GB | 100 GB |
| Bandwidth per month | 5 GB | 100 GB | 1 TB (soft) |
| Custom domains | 0 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Team members | 1 | 5 | 25 |
| Made with GO1CMS badge | Shown | Removable | Removable |
| Analytics retention | 30 days | 1 year | 2 years |
| Backups | daily (shared) | daily + download | daily + download + on demand |

Current prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## How limits are enforced

- **Pages, websites, domains and members** are checked when you add one; the builder tells you when a plan's cap is reached.
- **Storage** counts uploaded files.
- **Bandwidth** is estimated as published page weight times page views, plus API traffic. At 80 % you get an email; at 100 % uploads pause on Starter and Medium, and Starter pages switch to a light "over quota" page at 120 %. Premium is never cut.
- **CPU, disk and database** on your own site follow Frappe Cloud's plan limits.

## Idle Starter websites

A Starter website with no visits and no edits for 30 days is unpublished, and deleted after 90 days. You get three emails before that happens, and opening the builder resets the clock.

## Upgrading

Upgrade from the control panel. Medium and Premium start with a 14-day trial. Moving from Starter to Medium moves your project to a site of your own with the same addresses; you pick the moment.
