# SEO and analytics

## SEO

Every page has a title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph image and robots settings under **SEO**. Detail pages read these from their bound row, so `/products/solar-panel-450w` carries the product's own title.

The site serves a sitemap and robots file, server-renders every published page, and inlines the page's CSS so crawlers see the final layout. The SEO screen lists pages with missing titles or descriptions.

## Analytics

**Analytics** shows page views, visitors, sessions and top pages over a date range, with a real chart. Data is collected by a light script on published pages and kept for the retention period of your plan.

## Bandwidth

For plan limits, bandwidth is measured as the weight of each published page (HTML, CSS, images and fonts) times its views, plus API traffic. See [Plans and limits](/docs/plans-and-limits).
