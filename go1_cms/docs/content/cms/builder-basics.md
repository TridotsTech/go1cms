# Builder basics

The builder is a canvas with a left sidebar, a top bar and a right inspector.

## The canvas

- **Zoom and pan** like a design tool: pinch or `Cmd`/`Ctrl` + wheel to zoom around the cursor, hold `Space` and drag to pan.
- **Device switch** at the top shows desktop, tablet or phone. Styles you set while a device is selected apply to that device and smaller.
- **Select** by clicking. Text and headings select directly; containers select outermost first and drill in with further clicks. Double-click editable text to edit in place.
- **Rulers and padding handles** show measurements; drag a padding handle to change spacing.

## The left sidebar

- **Layers** is the page tree. Drag to reorder, click to select, toggle visibility.
- **Insert** (the **+** in the header) opens Templates, Sections and Elements.
- **Data** lists page variables, resources and events.
- **Animate** picks entrance and loop animations.
- **AI** rewrites copy or generates sections from a prompt.

## The inspector

The right panel is grouped like a design tool: Layout, Size, Position, Typography, Fill, Border, Effects, and a **Content** tab for element-specific settings. A selector at the top switches between the base style and hover, active and focus states.

## The top bar

Undo and redo, device switch, preview, share link, and **Publish**. Autosave keeps a draft as you work; nothing reaches your visitors until you publish.

## Keyboard

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Cmd`/`Ctrl` + `Z`, `Shift` + `Z` | Undo, redo |
| `Cmd`/`Ctrl` + `D` | Duplicate |
| `Delete` | Remove |
| `Space` + drag | Pan |
| `Cmd`/`Ctrl` + wheel | Zoom |
| `Cmd`/`Ctrl` + `Shift` + `V` | Paste a section from the library clipboard |
