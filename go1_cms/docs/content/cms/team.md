# Team and sharing

## Team members

Your account is a team. From **Team** in the control panel an Owner or Admin invites members by email. Roles:

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | everything, including billing and deleting the team |
| Admin | manage websites, members and settings |
| Editor | edit and publish pages |
| Viewer | open the builder read-only |

Members sign into your websites through the control panel. Removing a member removes their access everywhere.

## Share links

From the builder, **Share** creates a view-only preview link with a token. Anyone with the link can open the draft without an account. Revoke it from the same menu.

## Comments

Reviewers can leave comments on a shared preview; they appear in the builder next to the element.
